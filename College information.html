ABOUT OUR COLLEGE

NRI University, also known as NRI Institute of Technology, provides students with opportunities to learn, develop technical skills and prepare for their future careers.

Our aim is to encourage education, creativity, innovation and practical learning in a supportive academic environment.

The institution focuses on technical excellence, student development, placements and extracurricular activities.
